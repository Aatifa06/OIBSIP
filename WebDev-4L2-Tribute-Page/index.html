HIS STORY

From a small coastal town to the highest office in India.

Kalam's life is remembered not simply because of the positions he held, but because he connected science with service and ambition with responsibility.

01

Early Life & Education

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. His early years were shaped by a modest family life, discipline and a strong desire to learn. He later studied physics and then specialised in aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

Education became more than a qualification for him. It became the foundation for a life devoted to learning, experimentation and national development.

02

The Scientist

Kalam worked with India's space and defence research organisations for decades. At ISRO, he became closely associated with the SLV-III programme, whose successful 1980 mission placed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit.

03

Technology for India

At DRDO, Kalam took leadership roles connected with indigenous technology and the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme. His larger goal was self-reliance in critical technologies and the development of scientific capability within India.

04

The People's President

In 2002, Kalam became India's 11th President. During and after his presidency, he remained especially connected with students and young people, encouraging them to ask questions, build knowledge and imagine a stronger India.

05

The Teacher He Always Wanted to Be

After leaving the presidency in 2007, Kalam returned to teaching, writing and public interaction. He travelled widely to speak with students and researchers, treating education as a force for national transformation.

His final public engagement was also with students: he was delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong on 27 July 2015 when he collapsed and died.

A LIFE OF CONTRIBUTION

Science, leadership and service

01

Space Programme

His work at ISRO included leadership in the SLV-III programme and India's launch-vehicle development journey.

02

Defence Technology

At DRDO, he helped coordinate major technology programmes and strengthened India's indigenous research capabilities.

03

Technology Vision 2020

As Chairman of TIFAC, he helped shape a long-term vision focused on using technology and knowledge for national development.

04

Youth & Education

He made interaction with young people a central part of his public life and encouraged students to become responsible creators.

KEY MOMENTS

A journey marked by purpose

1931

Born in Rameswaram

Begins a journey that would take him from a coastal town to the Presidency of India.

1954

Physics Graduation

Completes his physics studies at St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.

1960

Aerospace Engineering

Completes aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology and enters scientific service.

1969

Moves to ISRO

Joins India's space programme and later becomes associated with the SLV-III project.

1980

Rohini Mission

The SLV-III successfully places the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit.

1992

Scientific Adviser

Becomes Scientific Adviser to the Defence Minister and Secretary of the Department of Defence R&D.

1997

Bharat Ratna

Receives India's highest civilian award for his contribution to scientific research and defence technology.

2002

11th President of India

Takes office on 25 July and becomes widely known as the People's President.

2007

Returns to Teaching

After his presidency, he continues teaching, writing and meeting students across India.

2015

Remembered as a Teacher

Dies on 27 July while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong, leaving behind a lasting educational legacy.

WHY HE IS REMEMBERED

His greatest legacy was not a title. It was a mindset.

Dr. Kalam showed that scientific ambition and humility could exist together. He spoke about national progress, but he also spoke about curiosity, learning, courage and responsibility. His ability to communicate complex ideas in an encouraging way made him especially influential among students.
